With learner storage configured, you can try these controls as you read.

## Take a note

Open **Notes** and type a reminder for yourself. Wait for **Saved**, then close the panel. Open it again to see your saved note for this lesson.

## Mark a lesson complete

Select **Mark complete** when you finish a lesson. The course overview counts your completed lessons.

## Resume reading

AutoDidact saves your reading position as you go. Return to **Course contents** and select **Continue reading** to pick up from the saved position.
